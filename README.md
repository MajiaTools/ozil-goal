## What it does

- **1 photo** → a 15-second reimagining of the famous November 2016 goal: chip the keeper, the pause, the turn, the finish
- Choose **720p or 1080p**, portrait framing; the template handles the action, timing and length — no music is added
- The complete-goal template supplies the action reference; other scenes use their own action instructions
- Sign-in is required; there is **no free generation allowance** — credits settle on measured duration and never exceed the confirmed quote
- Confirmed failed renders return your credits; a shorter valid video returns the unused difference

## How to use

1. Open https://ozil-goal.video
2. Upload one authorized adult photo (JPEG/PNG, up to 10 MB; clear, evenly lit face — a full-body photo gives the model more to work with) and pick your scene and resolution
3. Review the credit quote, generate, and follow the task in My videos; download the MP4 when complete
